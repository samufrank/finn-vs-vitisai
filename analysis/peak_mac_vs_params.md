- LUT: tiny (17,930), small (35,706), deep_3 (59,981), medium (80,753), large (158,294)

- total_fabric_mac: tiny (225,792), small (903,168), deep_3 (1,806,336), medium (3,612,672), large (7,225,344)

The params order inverts against the LUT order at medium/deep_3: deep_3 has more
params than medium but less LUT. The peak_mac order has no inversion against LUT,
but ties small with deep_3 and medium with large. total_fabric_mac and sum_pe_simd
are strictly monotonic with LUT.

## Per-layer folding and resource

OFM positions = MAC / weight_params. weight_params = MW*MH = K^2 * Cin * Cout,
which has no spatial term, so equal-width convs at different resolutions have
equal weight_params but different MAC.

| arch | layer | MAC | weight_params | OFM | PE | SIMD | PE*SIMD | fold | synth_LUT | synth_DSP | DSP==PE*SIMD | peak |
|------|-------|----:|--------------:|-----|---:|-----:|--------:|-----:|----------:|----------:|:-----------:|:----:|
| tiny | MVAU_hls_0 | 225,792 | 1,152 | 14x14 | 1 | 3 | 3 | 384 | 5187 | 3 | yes | yes |
| small | MVAU_hls_0 | 903,168 | 4,608 | 14x14 | 4 | 4 | 16 | 288 | 18818 | 16 | yes | yes |
| medium | MVAU_hls_0 | 3,612,672 | 18,432 | 14x14 | 16 | 4 | 64 | 288 | n/a (bust) | - | - | yes |
| deep_3 | MVAU_hls_0 | 903,168 | 4,608 | 14x14 | 4 | 4 | 16 | 288 | 20380 | 16 | yes | yes |
| deep_3 | MVAU_hls_1 | 903,168 | 18,432 | 7x7 | 4 | 4 | 16 | 1152 | 21529 | 16 | yes | yes |
| large | MVAU_hls_0 | 3,612,672 | 18,432 | 14x14 | 16 | 4 | 64 | 288 | n/a (bust) | - | - | yes |
| large | MVAU_hls_1 | 3,612,672 | 73,728 | 7x7 | 16 | 4 | 64 | 1152 | n/a (bust) | - | - | yes |

medium MVAU_hls_0 and deep_3 MVAU_hls_1 are both 32 to 64 channel convs with the
same weight_params (18,432), but run at 14x14 vs 7x7, so 4x the MAC, PE 16 vs 4,
and bust vs fit. SIMD is capped at 4 for INT8 (weight_bits * SIMD <= 36). DSP equals
PE*SIMD on every fit MVAU, confirming the folding config matches the synthesized design.
